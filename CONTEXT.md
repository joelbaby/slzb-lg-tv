# Domain glossary

- **TV**: the single LG webOS television configured in an app installation.
- **Coordinator**: the SMLIGHT device hosting and running the app.
- **Pairing**: the user-approved exchange that grants the app permission to control the TV.
- **Pairing key**: the credential returned by the TV after approval; retained privately by the app.
- **Forget**: delete this app's saved pairing key. This does not imply revoking authorization on the TV.
- **Command result**: whether an action was submitted or failed. It is distinct from confirmed TV power state.
